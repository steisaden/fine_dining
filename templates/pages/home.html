The chef’s practice

Cook the ingredient clearly.
Stop before explanation becomes decoration.

Esker is a fictional chef-led dining practice built around seasonal tasting menus and attentive service for small tables. Menus respond to availability, setting, dietary needs, and the desired pace of the evening.

The work happens in the details guests should not have to manage: sequence, temperature, space, accessibility, and the handover between kitchen and table.

Considered as a complete evening, not a reduced restaurant service.
