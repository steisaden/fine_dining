Before / during / after

The work around service.

  1. Before

    Listen and map

    We discuss the occasion, kitchen, location, timing, guest needs, allergies, accessibility, and the atmosphere you want.

  2. During

    Cook and recede

    The service is paced to the room. Where practical, we manage setup and the movement between kitchen and table quietly.

  3. After

    Leave the room settled

    Service concludes with an agreed handover. Site-specific logistics and responsibilities are confirmed before the date.
