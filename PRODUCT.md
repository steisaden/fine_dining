# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Guests considering a seasonal tasting, a private dinner, a chef’s-table evening, a small celebration, or a culinary collaboration.

## Product Purpose

Esker turns the final millimeter of plating into an intimate editorial introduction to chef-led hospitality. Visitors should understand the offer, explore the current menu, and submit a considered inquiry without encountering invented credentials or manufactured urgency.

## Positioning

The film is the site’s physical world. Course notation, fine rules, and restrained typographic movement behave like service marks around porcelain rather than conventional restaurant marketing.

## Evidence and constraints

- Supplied 1920×1080, 60fps H.264 film with AAC audio; duration is read from metadata.
- No awards, ratings, testimonials, press, prices, provenance claims, or confirmation claims.
- Seasonal menu and service descriptions are editorial examples for the fictional brand.
- Every route and form remains usable without JavaScript.

## Product principles

1. The film leads; interface annotation never competes with the plate.
2. Hospitality is specific, calm, and subject to availability.
3. Progressive enhancement must preserve the complete path.
4. The final course resolves into quieter document flow.

## Accessibility

Semantic landmarks, visible focus, 44px targets, server-side validation, focus-managed swaps, reduced-motion document mode, and high-contrast backplates where film contrast varies.
